01 · Matchday · Cars vs robots

Bendy
Fenders

New · Nov 2026

Football, but everyone's a car. The ball plays like pool: hit it dead centre and it flies straight, clip it off-centre and it cuts away. Every goal is a shot you lined up.

You drive with one finger. Put it down anywhere and drag, and the car goes that way. A second finger boosts. Let go to brake.

  • 01Story50 puzzle levels in five chapters, best time on each
  • 02Match1 vs 1, 2 vs 2 or 1 vs 2 against robots, golden goal on a draw
  • 03GarageTen paint jobs, paid for with coins you win

02 · Arcade · One tap

Satisfying Circles Deluxe

A ball bounces inside rings that spin and shrink. Get the gap under the ball before the ring closes in on it.

Tap the left side to push the inner ring forward, the right side to send it back. Every ring you clear brings a new one on the outside, and everything gets a little faster.

  • Four looks: Classic, Monochrome, Pastel, Neon
  • Ball skins, bought with coins from your runs
  • Five to ten rings, you pick the pressure
  • One revive per run, if you want to watch an ad for it
Get it on Google Play

03 · Puzzle · Pixel art

Sliding Penguins

Penguins on ice don't stop where you'd like them to.

Swipe, and every penguin slides until it hits a wall or another penguin. Plan the order, use each other as brakes, and get them where they need to go. It starts gentle and gets tricky.

House rules

What every game here has to pass before it ships.

  1. You get it in a minute. If it needs a manual, it isn't done.
  2. It plays offline. On the bus, in the tunnel, on the plane. No account to make.
  3. Your progress stays on your phone. No servers of mine are keeping it.
  4. Coins come from playing. In Bendy Fenders and Circles, there's nothing to buy with real money.
  5. Ads stay out of the way of play. They keep the games free. They never pop up in the middle of a shot.
